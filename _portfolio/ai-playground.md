## AI Playground

This is the space for small AI experiments: prototypes, agents, interface ideas, and workflow automations. The point is not only to make something clever, but to make something that feels natural to use.

### Direction

- AI-assisted personal tools
- Web interfaces with low friction
- Automation experiments
- Product ideas that can grow from weekend prototypes
